Our Story

More than
a dance studio.
A community.

Since 2006, Rumpuree has been Bangkok's home for world dance — a place where beginners discover their confidence and seasoned dancers refine their artistry. We believe dance transcends age, background, and experience level.

With over 45 dance styles, internationally certified instructors, and a warm, inclusive atmosphere, we have earned our place as one of Thailand's premier dance institutions.
